# 句级干扰项样本（30 条）

误译模式限定三类：实词误解 / 虚词功能丢失或误判 / 特殊句式误读。

### 1. [被动句] 有如此之势，而为秦人积威之所劫，日削月割，以趋于亡。

正确：`With such an advantage, yet terrorised by Qin's accumulated might, they were pared daily, carved monthly, drifting toward ruin.`

| # | 干扰项 | 错误类型 | 成因 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `With such an advantage, yet they terrorised Qin's accumulated might, paring and carving land day by day, drift…` | 特殊句式误读 | 「为秦人积威之所劫」被动未还原，劫掠方向反转：六国由被胁迫者变成施动者。 |
| 2 | `With such an advantage, yet kidnapped by Qin's accumulated might, they were pared daily, carved monthly, drift…` | 实词误解 | 「劫」＝胁迫、挟制（国势被挟制），不是绑架（kidnap）。 |
| 3 | `With such an advantage, yet terrorised by Qin's accumulated might, they were pared daily and carved monthly be…` | 虚词功能丢失/误判 | 「以趋于亡」的「以」表结果（以致于走向灭亡），不是表原因的 because。 |

### 2. [被动句] 一夫作难而七庙隳，身死人手，为天下笑者，何也？

正确：`yet when one man rose in revolt the ancestral temples crumbled, its ruler died at another's hand, a laughing-stock to the world. Why?`

| # | 干扰项 | 错误类型 | 成因 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `yet when one man rose in revolt he destroyed the seven ancestral temples and killed the ruler with his own han…` | 特殊句式误读 | 「为天下笑」被动未还原，被译成陈涉主动施暴；「身死人手」的施受关系随之改写。 |
| 2 | `yet when one man rose in revolt the seven ancestral temples were burned down, its ruler died at another's hand…` | 实词误解 | 「隳」＝毁坏、倾覆，译作纵火属无据增译，陈涉起事并未焚庙。 |
| 3 | `yet he who rose in revolt, whose temples crumbled, who died at another's hand and was laughed at by the world …` | 虚词功能丢失/误判 | 「为天下笑者，何也」的「者」是提顿（……的原因），被误作定语「的人」，主语由「秦亡」错移到作难者本人。 |

### 23. [疑问句] 然则何时而乐耶？

### 24. [疑问句] 微斯人，吾谁与归？
